What I do in your account

Research and build

I look at what customers search for, what competitors offer and how your business makes money. Then I build campaigns, write ads and set budgets around what we want to learn or sell.

Measure what pays

I check what counts as a conversion, how it’s recorded and whether it reflects a real sale or useful enquiry. Where the data allows, I connect campaign performance to orders or closed deals.

Test and improve

I review search terms, bids and budgets, test ad copy and landing pages, and put more budget behind campaigns that have shown they can turn it into profitable business.

Make the next click count

I look at whether the landing page delivers what the ad promised, how easy it is to enquire or buy, and where customers get stuck on the way to checkout or a sales call.

What I often find when I open an account

Ads showing for the wrong searches

A campaign can look busy while paying for searches from people who want something you don’t sell. Search terms tell us what people actually typed and where negative keywords can help.

Conversions that count the wrong things

Duplicate tags, button clicks counted as leads and missing purchase values can all steer bidding towards the wrong outcome. I trace a conversion back to what actually happened.

Campaigns and budgets split too thin

When each campaign sees only a handful of meaningful conversions, automated bidding has less evidence about what works. I look for places to combine related campaigns while keeping separate budgets where the business needs them.

Google Ads questions

Who will I work with?

Me. I’ll be the person on the call and in the account. I do the strategy, the account work and the reporting myself.

Do you work with e-commerce and lead generation?

Yes. With a shop, I want to understand revenue, margins and repeat purchases where we can measure them. With lead generation, I want to know which enquiries are qualified, which close and what those jobs are worth.

Can you work with the agency or team I already have?

Yes. I can take responsibility for the account or work on an agreed part of it alongside your team. We’ll agree who owns which decisions before work starts.

Do I need to give you access before the first call?

No. We can talk about the business and your goals first. If it makes sense to do a review afterwards, I’ll ask for read-only Google Ads access.

What’s the difference between a review and an audit?

The complimentary review gives you a first look at the account and the issues I’d investigate. A full paid audit sets aside time to check tracking, structure, targeting, budgets and the path to a sale in depth. You get detailed findings and a prioritised plan.

How much time will my account need?

That depends on the number of campaigns, markets, products and the state of the tracking, as well as what you want to improve. We’ll agree the time and fee up front. If something needs more time, I’ll bring it to you before doing extra work.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

My fee is based on the time we agree each month. You pay Google for your ads directly. The management fee only changes when we agree to change the time or scope.

How quickly can you improve results?

I can often spot tracking or targeting problems early. Measuring the effect of changes takes longer and depends on how much data the account sees and how long customers take to buy.

Can I hire you for an audit or a rebuild only?

Yes. We can agree a one-off scope for an audit, a new account build or a rebuild, then hand it back to you to manage.
