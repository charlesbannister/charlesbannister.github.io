Software questions

How do you decide what to build?

I’ll ask you to walk me through the job as it works today, including the exceptions. We’ll agree what a first version needs to do and how we’ll know it works.

Can you build it yourself or work with my team?

Yes. I’ve been programming for over 15 years, most of that time professionally. I’ve built software for teams including GoDaddy’s paid media team. I can handle the build myself or work alongside your designer or developer.

Can you improve something we already use?

Yes. I can look at your existing script, spreadsheet or application and work out whether to repair it, extend it or replace the parts that are holding it back.

How do you handle errors in an automation?

I look at what happens if a feed stops arriving, the data is wrong or an update only partly succeeds. We agree where a preview, a log or an alert is useful so somebody can see what happened.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. We can agree what I hand over and whether you’d like ongoing support. I document the parts you’ll need to run and maintain it.

Let’s see if we’re a good fit.

Tell me what you want to improve. We’ll talk through what you’ve tried, what’s getting in the way and where I can help.
