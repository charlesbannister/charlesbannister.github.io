A few questions you might have

Will you be the person doing the work?

Yes. You’ll speak directly with me and I’ll do the work. I can also work alongside your existing team.

Can I hire you for just one part of this?

Yes. I can manage Google Ads, build a site or landing page, or make a tool for a specific job. If the pieces need to work together, I can take on the whole project.

How much does ongoing management cost?

We agree how much of my time you need and what it costs each month. Account complexity and what we’re tackling determine the time. The arrangement runs month to month and the fee is based on my time.

Is the account review an audit?

The complimentary review gives you my first impressions after an introductory call. If you want an account-wide investigation with detailed findings and a plan, we can scope a paid audit.
