Areas of expertise

After working freelance for a total of ~11 years my projects have been all over the shop. It keeps me on my toes. I sit somewhere between marketing and software, and most of my best work happens where the two meet. Here are the major threads:

Marketing & Analytics

  • Paid search & paid media

    I started out as a PPC Executive, and I've never really left. Google Ads is home turf, and I've run Display, Affiliates, Retargeting and paid social on Meta alongside it.

    I've managed Google Ads accounts (amongst other things) consistently since ~2011. You can't beat the buzz of watching those numbers improve!

  • Tracking & analytics

    If the numbers are wrong, everything built on them is wrong too. I've set up a lot of conversion tracking (server side, or otherwise) with Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, and I'm happy digging into why two reports disagree.

    Then there's the reporting itself: Looker Studio dashboards, Google Sheets with Apps Script doing the heavy lifting, and plenty of analysis besides.

  • E-commerce & CRO

    As an E-commerce Manager I got to see the other side of it all: margins, stock, staffing, ROI, scaling, the lot. I ran A/B tests (CRO) and learnt that a good campaign is only as good as the page and the product behind it.

  • CRM & marketing automation

    HubSpot, email and the plumbing between them. Getting leads from the ad, to the CRM, to someone's inbox without anything falling through the cracks.

Software

  • Website & Web App Development

    This is where it all began, and it continues to this day. My degree is in "Interactive Digital Media" which was essentially a Web & Graphic design degree. On the backend I've worked with Laravel & Wordpress (PHP), Express (Node.js), FastAPI, Django & Flask (Python). For the shop window, I've written a lot of vanilla JavaScript/TypeScript, but also worked extensively with libraries like Vue & React.

    I'm ok with CSS, but if you're looking for a slick, well designed UI/UX I am not your man.

  • Cloud & DevOps

    I have a decent amount of Google Cloud experience (APIs, BigQuery) however, I've used AWS far more. Within AWS, I rely on a lot of the classics: EC2, S3, Lambda, ECS/Fargate, SQS, SNS, WAF, CloudFormation, and more acronyms besides!

    I've also worked with AWS's Step Functions as a workflow orchestration tool, as well as Prefect and Airflow.

    Whilst I don't claim to be a DevOps expert, CI/CD via GitHub actions, Pre-commit checks, linting, automated testing, strong observability, etc. are a large part of every project I work on.

  • Data and automation

    I got a job in PPC and immediately had access to all of this data! It needed processing, turning into reports, analysis, etc. and I reached immediately for Python. I've worked with Python (including Pandas, NumPy, Polars, etc.) every since - going on ten years.

    As well as workflow orchestration, I've worked with Data Lakes and Data Warehouses, AWS Athena & RedShift, BigQuery, as well as DuckDB/Parquet workflows.
