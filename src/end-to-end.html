Questions about end-to-end work

Do I need to rebuild my site or Google Ads account?

We can start with what you have. I’ll look at the campaign, page and tracking together and suggest the smallest changes that address the problem.

Can you help track what happens after a lead comes in?

Yes. We can look at how your team records qualified leads, quotes and closed deals, then see what can be linked back to the campaign. Where the data is patchy, I’ll explain what we can measure reliably.

What would we look at for an online shop?

Orders and revenue are a starting point. Where the data is available, I’ll look at margins and which products can support more ad spend.

Can you work with our sales team or web developer?

Yes. We’ll agree who owns each part and what information we need to share so we can see where sales are coming from and where they get stuck.
