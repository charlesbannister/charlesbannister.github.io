Why use a washer disinfector?

One controlled process, less manual handling.

Washer disinfectors clean and thermally disinfect instruments before sterilisation. They help reduce operator exposure, create consistent repeatable cycles and give your practice a clearer decontamination workflow.

Place instruments in the correct basket

02 / RUN

Move on to packaging

Drying and cycle records support the next stage of your sterilisation workflow.

Still weighing up which option is right? Start here, then ask us for a recommendation.

Does a washer disinfector replace an autoclave?

No. A washer disinfector cleans and thermally disinfects instruments before sterilisation. Instruments still need to go through the appropriate sterilisation process afterwards.

What is the difference between the D60 and T60?

Both are underbench Tethys washer disinfectors with cycle traceability and flexible programmes. The D60 adds a dedicated hot-air drying system with HEPA filtration, while the T60 uses thermal drying with the door opening at the end of the cycle.

Can you help with installation and servicing?

Yes. Installation, commissioning, training, validation and ongoing service can be discussed as part of your quote, depending on the model and your site requirements.
