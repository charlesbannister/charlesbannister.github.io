From the first call to a decision

1. Free consultation

We’ll talk about your business, where you want to get to and what’s getting in the way. For ad accounts, I’ll ask how a click turns into revenue and what you can measure today.

2. Complimentary account review

If Google Ads is part of the conversation, I’ll take a light look after the call and send a short video with what I noticed, what I’d check next and where I’d start.

3. Optional follow-up call

We’ll talk through the findings if you’d like. If we both think it’s a good fit, I’ll set out what I’d do, the time involved and the price.

How ongoing work is priced

We agree a monthly fee for a set amount of my time. The hours reflect the complexity of the account and what you need help with. I tackle the most important work first within that time and tell you if something needs more hours before proceeding.

Ongoing work is month to month. There’s no long-term commitment and the fee is based on the hours we agree. For a one-off project or full audit, we’ll agree the scope and price up front.

Questions about working together

What should I bring to the first call?

A rough idea of what you want to improve and what you’ve tried. If it’s about Google Ads, it’s helpful to know how much you spend, what a customer is worth and where you think things are going wrong.

What if I need more than an initial review?

We can scope a paid audit. That gives me time to work through the account and tracking properly, verify what I find and give you a detailed list of what to fix first.

Can we talk through the review live?

Yes. I send the video so you can watch when it suits you. If you’d like to discuss it or ask questions, we’ll set up another call.

What if my priorities change mid-month?

Tell me and we’ll reorder the work within the hours we’ve agreed. If the new work needs more time, we’ll discuss what to move back or whether to add time.

Can you help if I already have an in-house team?

Yes. I’m happy to work with the people who know your business and agree which parts each of us will handle.
