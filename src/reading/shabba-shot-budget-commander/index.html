Budget commander

November 20, 2026

Imagine it’s halfway through the month and your client asks whether the remaining budget can cover a weekend promotion. You need the month-to-date total, an estimate of where it’s heading, and a plan you can explain across campaigns.

Who needs it, and when?

Lead with the person accountable for the monthly number, such as an agency lead who spends Friday reconciling budgets. Their spreadsheet can hold a plan. Show the value of opening the plan beside actual spend and a forecast in the same review.

The strongest story is a decision made early enough to explain to the client. Use the automation as a separate content angle for buyers who can say exactly which campaigns may pause and under what conditions. A buyer who wants advice alone can start with pacing and the plan.

What the Shot does for them

Spend and budget across chosen accounts
Spot which client accounts need a pacing conversation.
Month-end forecast with spend to date
Give the team an early estimate they can compare with the agreed budget and upcoming events.
Campaign allocations that sum to the monthly account plan
See the effect on the rest of the account when you give one campaign more room.
Configurable pause and re-enable rules with logged activity
Agree what should happen at a spending threshold and check the record when an enabled automation acts.

Draft page copy

If this pace continues, where will the month land?

See where Google Ads spend is heading this month, then plan what each campaign can use. Keep the total in view as you adjust allocations, and set a pause rule if the account needs one.

What to show in a demo

  1. Use illustrative spend for two accounts, with one forecast above its agreed monthly budget.
  2. Open that account’s plan and show how giving the promotion more room affects the other allocations.
  3. Show the setup of an optional pause rule and the paused log using sample data.

Content angles

The mid-month budget conversation to have today
Put the current spend, forecast and next decision on one screen in a pacing walkthrough.
Before you turn on budget pauses
Use an FAQ to explain when the rule checks, which campaigns it can pause, and how re-enabling works.

Questions a buyer might ask

Can I use the pacing and plan without automatic pauses?
Yes. Review spend and forecasts and use the plan on its own. Enable pause rules only where they suit the account.

The forecast estimates month-end spend. An enabled pause rule is evaluated at each check, and spend can pass the Shot’s threshold between checks.
