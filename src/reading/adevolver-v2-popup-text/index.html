Your next step: AdEvolver V2

We've got you well covered with V2's dedicated, intelligent alerts, including a new breach detection system.

Three cards

  • Breach detection
    Ad links switching to an unfamiliar domain, or new admins added to Google Ads.
  • Critical alerts
    Conversions stopping while ads still get clicks, or your busiest landing pages going down.
  • Delivery you control
    Choose which alerts reach you by email and which stay in the app.

What changes when you move

  • You have [number] alerts. They'll be paused in favour of the alerts in the new version, so you don't get too many alert emails.
    Only if the team has alerts.
  • Budget Commander alerts move too. Your [number] Budget Commander alerts will be paused. Set up the alerts you want in V2's Budget Commander.
    Only if the team has Budget Commander alerts.
  • Make changes in V2. Add or remove negative keywords, exclude placements, pause or enable campaigns and change campaign budgets. You can't yet add keywords or change ads, links or bids there.
  • Automatic campaign pausing stays here. If Budget Commander pauses campaigns for you, it carries on, and you can still change it in this version.
  • Your whole team moves together. Everyone on the team will see V2 the next time they sign in.
  • No Slack notifications in V2 yet. If you relied on them, let us know and we'll add them.

Give it a try. You can switch back at any time.

A note on AI

AI is on by default, which means your account data is sent to Anthropic. Through Anthropic's API, your data isn't used to train models and is deleted within 30 days (longer only if flagged for misuse). You can turn AI off for each account.
