Campaign and ad group trends

November 20, 2026

A client says the phone went quiet after an otherwise routine week. In an illustrative account, brand campaigns held steady while one non-brand campaign dropped. Within that campaign, the movement is concentrated in a particular ad group. That’s a more useful starting point than an account-wide percentage.

Who needs it, and when?

Speak to the PPC lead with enough campaigns that an account average no longer explains anything. The alternatives are jumping between Google Ads views or maintaining a reporting spreadsheet. The decision is which campaign or group earns a closer look before a broad account change.

Anchor the pitch in a call about one account. Account trends tells the agency lead which account to open. This Shot helps the practitioner narrow down what moved inside it. Show the hierarchy and the date of the shift together so the viewer can connect the report to the actual work.

What the Shot does for them

Campaigns, ad groups and Performance Max asset groups
Follow a change to the group where it is concentrated and give the person who owns it something concrete to investigate.
Weeks or months with the comparable periods a year earlier
Compare when the movement began and add seasonal context before changing budgets or targets.
Campaign views by channel, device and bid strategy, plus Search impression share where reported
Start with the split most likely to explain where delivery changed and use budget or rank loss to frame the next check.

Draft page copy

Leads fell. Which campaign should you open first?

Find where the change happened inside your Google Ads account. Compare campaigns, ad groups and Performance Max asset groups over the same weeks or months. Follow the movement to the group that needs attention and bring the relevant impression-share figures into the conversation.

What to show in a demo

  1. Set up an illustrative account where conversions fall while its brand campaign holds steady.
  2. Compare campaigns by week. Use the campaign-name filter for the affected campaign and switch to the Ad groups view.
  3. Look at the relevant Search impression-share figures where available. Close with the next account check you would make.

Content angles

One slow week, one ad group to check
Show the path from the campaign comparison to the ad group. Lead with this because it proves the value in one short scene.
Before you move the budget
Use a pair of campaigns with different delivery histories to explain why the same conversion trend can send you to different checks.

Questions a buyer might ask

Does it cover Performance Max?
Yes. The Shot includes Performance Max asset groups.
Will every row have Search impression share?
Use it for campaigns and ad groups where Google reports it. Coverage varies with the campaign and data available.
