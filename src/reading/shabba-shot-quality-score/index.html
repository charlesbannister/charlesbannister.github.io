Quality score

November 20, 2026

Imagine two campaigns with low-scoring keywords. A few pounds went through one. The other accounts for a noticeable share of spend. The second is the place to start looking, especially if one component of the score points you towards a test.

Who needs it, and when?

Start with the PPC specialist preparing an account review. They can already add Quality Score columns in Google Ads. What they need here is a view of how the diagnostic lines up with spend, then a way to narrow the investigation to one campaign.

Sell the decision that follows the chart. If landing page experience stands out, show the ad and landing page together and look for a mismatch. When impact is unknown, suggest a test and measure business outcomes separately.

What the Shot does for them

Spend grouped by Quality Score, including unscored spend
Put the low scores in context and see how much of the account’s spend sits outside scored keywords.
Spend by expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience
Identify which part of the experience merits a closer look.
Weekly and campaign views
Find the campaign behind the account-level picture and compare its score over time.

Draft page copy

Which low Quality Scores are worth investigating?

See where your keyword spend sits across Google’s Quality Scores. Break the result down by campaign and by expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience, then choose where to look first.

What to show in a demo

  1. Start on a sample account with different spend levels across low-scoring campaigns.
  2. Go from the spend distribution to the campaign view and inspect the weakest component.
  3. Open the relevant ad and landing page outside the Shot and name one improvement worth testing.

Content angles

A low score on a keyword nobody clicks
Put two sample campaigns side by side and ask which deserves an hour of work first.
Three scores, three different next steps
Create a reference card with an illustrative diagnosis for each component and the evidence that would support further work.

Questions a buyer might ask

What does the unscored spend represent?
It is spend included in the report where Google has supplied no keyword Quality Score. Keep it visible when explaining coverage.

Use Quality Score to choose a campaign or landing page to investigate. Measure business outcomes separately if you make a change.
