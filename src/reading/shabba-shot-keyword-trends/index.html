Keyword trends

Labs · November 20, 2026

Imagine a Search manager with a keyword that reliably brings enquiries. This month it has slipped. The next decision is where to look first. Has the keyword been showing less often, has its reported quality picture changed, or is the work elsewhere?

Who needs it, and when?

Lead with valuable, established Search keywords. A campaign total may conceal the movement, and a screenshot of today’s Quality Score cannot show when it began. The buyer’s alternative is switching between reports or keeping a separate history of the keywords that matter.

Sell the ability to choose a better next check. Put visibility and performance across the same dates, then use the Quality Score components as clues. That can turn “leads are down” into a narrower conversation with the person responsible for the ad or landing page.

What the Shot does for them

Keyword performance by week or month, including a year-earlier comparison
Put a date on the movement and see what was typical at a comparable time before deciding whether to act.
Search impression share and measures for rank loss, top and absolute top
Separate a question about visibility from the question of what happened after somebody clicked.
Quality Score history and the three reported components
See whether expected click-through rate, ad relevance or landing page experience gives you a lead worth following.

Draft page copy

When did that dependable keyword start losing visibility?

Follow the Search keywords you rely on week by week. See when delivery and results moved, compared with the same period last year. Put impression share alongside the keyword’s Quality Score history to work out where to investigate next.

What to show in a demo

  1. Choose an illustrative Search keyword with a steady baseline and a recent drop in visibility.
  2. Point out the week the movement began and compare it with the same time last year.
  3. Open the reported Quality Score components for that keyword and show which signal would shape your next manual check.

Content angles

Your best keyword went quiet
Tell the story of one term using three views in order. Start with this because the owner can picture why they would run the report.
Before and after a landing-page change
Use an illustrative keyword history to show what you would look for in the weeks around a page change. End with the on-page and tracking checks needed to support any conclusion.

Questions a buyer might ask

Does this show what the customer typed?
This view follows the keywords in the Ads account. Use Search term trends to follow the reported queries people typed.
What does the Quality Score value for a week represent?
The latest historical value Google reported for that week, where available.

Impression share and Quality Score history are diagnostic evidence. A change in either can prompt a check of bids, ads or pages alongside other account context.
