Product trends

Labs · November 20, 2026

An ecommerce lead sees Shopping return on ad spend fall and asks whether to cut back. In an illustrative account, a clearance range has taken more of the spend while the core range is still steady. That distinction changes who the marketer needs to talk to before touching a campaign.

Who needs it, and when?

Lead with the weekly call between paid media and merchandising. The PPC manager needs to say which part of the catalogue to check. The merchandising lead needs something they can recognise and act on, such as a product type, range or item ID.

The practical alternative is pulling a Shopping report and matching product exports across dates. A good demo earns interest by moving from the affected range to the specific items someone can investigate. The most useful next step might be to check stock, margins, pricing or what was on promotion. The report supplies the advertising evidence for that conversation.

Draft page copy

Which products are pulling down your Shopping return?

See which parts of your catalogue changed week by week. Compare Shopping and Performance Max performance by brand, label or product type, then look at the individual items driving the movement. Bring that shortlist to your next conversation with merchandising.

What to show in a demo

  1. Open an illustrative account after a promotion and select the months around it.
  2. Compare the account’s advertised products by custom label, then open the item ID view to identify the most relevant products.
  3. Show the same month a year earlier and end with one item to check against stock or pricing records.

Content angles

Shopping ROAS fell. What changed in the catalogue?
Build a short walkthrough from a product-group change to the item IDs to review. Try this angle first because it links the report to a budget decision.
What did the sale leave behind?
Compare the featured range before, during and after a promotion. Show how the weeks help frame the next conversation with merchandising.

Questions a buyer might ask

Can I follow a SKU if its product title changes?
Use the item ID view when the underlying ID stays the same. That view can be limited to the products with the most spend in the selected dates.
Does this calculate profit?
The report reflects Google Ads performance. Bring your own margin and fulfilment figures when the decision depends on profit.

Product changes give you a lead. Bring in inventory, feed or commercial data to establish why a product moved.
