AdEvolver V2: every email it sends

The current wording of all 40 automated and transactional emails in V2, as plain text, filled with sample details (Northstar, Alex, example links). Each one says whether it is sent today and whether its wording can be changed in Admin → Automated emails.

Sign-in and security · Team access · Getting started · Early access · Billing · Support · Notifications · Legacy budget automation · Operations · Outage monitor (outside the app)

Before you read

  • Where to change wording: Admin → Automated emails. 19 emails are editable on production now. The other 19 app emails are editable in the local build and go live with the next release.
  • The wording shown is the default. If anyone has already saved changes on production, those win. Two emails (support received, daily alert summary) have small wording changes in the local build; the local version is shown.
  • Some lines only appear in certain situations, for example the "Slack is not available" paragraph, which only shows for people who had chosen Slack.
  • Not included: Stripe's own receipts and card emails, and Classic's emails.

Things that stood out

  • Four subjects still read like the old Laravel app: "Reset Password Notification", "Verify Email Address", "Your AdEvolver Invoice", "AdEvolver account access invitation accepted". "Access revoked" is blunter than the heading inside it ("Workspace access removed").
  • The immediate alert says "1 more findings".
  • The legacy budget emails show money as "£100.0" while the newer ones use "GBP 5,000". The restart emails end with "Thanks for choosing AdEvolver."
  • The trial welcome is thin: a trial end date and "see the latest result", with nothing on what to try first.
  • Both beta emails promise that Classic alerts pause or resume. That needs the per-team switch before either can go out.

Welcome to AdEvolver V2

Your workspace is now on the AdEvolver V2 beta. Your Classic alerts and notifications are paused while your workspace is on V2, and your Classic settings are saved.

Breach detection can flag changes such as ad links switching to an unfamiliar domain or a new Google Ads admin. Critical alerts can flag problems such as conversions stopping while ads still get clicks. You can customise the checks or create alerts for your own conditions and schedules.

You can switch back at any time. In V2, choose Back to Classic. Your Classic alerts will resume using your saved settings, and V2 alert delivery will pause. We’ll email you to confirm the switch.

V2 is in early beta. If something looks wrong or you have feedback, email support@adevolver.com.
