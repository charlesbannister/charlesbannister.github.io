Search term tamer

November 20, 2026

Imagine reviewing a week’s search terms for a business that installs commercial equipment. A query about a second-hand part may be irrelevant. A model number you’ve never heard of may be from exactly the right buyer. The Shot should help you see the difference before you exclude anything.

Who needs it, and when?

Lead with a PPC manager doing a recurring search-term review. The real alternative is filtering in Google Ads, using a script or putting the review off. Show what your configured rules catch and how the workflow preserves account knowledge.

A no-conversion row is a prompt to investigate. Timing, a small sample and unfamiliar language can all make a good search look suspect. A demo with a term you keep is as persuasive as a demo with a term you exclude.

What the Shot does for them

Rules over reported Search, Shopping and Performance Max terms
Give your review a repeatable starting point with criteria such as costly clicks and no recorded conversions.
Protected words and keywords
Keep the commercial searches you already know matter out of the shortlist when they match your protections.
Optional AI analysis of selected terms
Get another interpretation of an ambiguous search while keeping the decision with the person who knows the business.
Selected exact or phrase negatives
Choose the scope of the exclusion and review the change before applying it.

Draft page copy

Find the searches worth ruling out

Give your next search-term review a starting point. Use your rules to surface terms worth checking, protect the words that matter to the account, and choose which exact or phrase negatives to add.

What to show in a demo

  1. Open a sample report with a clearly irrelevant query and a promising but unfamiliar model number.
  2. Show which rule brought the terms into view, and open optional AI analysis on the ambiguous row if it is available.
  3. Keep the promising term and show the proposed exclusion for the irrelevant one before it is applied.

Content angles

Would you exclude this search?
Use three illustrative rows as a short quiz. Reveal what additional context changes the answer.
Build a Friday search-term routine
Record one pass from rule to review to the proposed exact or phrase negative.

Questions a buyer might ask

Does it add negatives on its own?
The Shot lets you select the changes to send for review. The rule results and optional AI analysis are inputs to that decision.
