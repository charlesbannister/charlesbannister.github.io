Page speed

Labs · November 20, 2026

Imagine the web team can fit one landing-page performance fix into this sprint. There are dozens of URLs in the ad account. Put the PageSpeed results beside paid spend and make the request about a page customers actually reach.

Who needs it, and when?

Start with a paid media lead who relies on a separate web team. They could paste URLs into PageSpeed Insights manually. What they gain from this Shot is the Ads context required to say which landing page belongs at the front of that work queue.

Show the content that took a while to appear and why it matters to the buyer. For a quote page that could be the form or the message the ad promised. A score belongs in the supporting detail.

What the Shot does for them

PageSpeed Insights tests in spend order
Prioritise slow destinations based on paid exposure and narrow a long page list to a practical shortlist.
Mobile or desktop testing and lab timings
Investigate the experience on the relevant device and identify a concrete loading issue to reproduce.
Chrome real-visitor data, where Google has enough
Check whether you have visitor evidence alongside the lab run.
PageSpeed recommendations for individual URLs
Give the developer a starting point and an affected URL they can open and test.

Draft page copy

Which paid landing page should your developer fix first?

Bring paid traffic into your landing-page speed review. Test ad destinations with Google PageSpeed Insights, start with high-spend pages, and give your web team a clearer shortlist.

What to show in a demo

  1. In sample data, choose two pages with different spend and mobile speed profiles.
  2. Open the higher-priority page and inspect a lab timing and one recommendation.
  3. Show how you’d write the request for one fix and how you’d test that page again after it ships.

Content angles

Turn a speed score into a ticket someone can pick up
Use a short screen recording to show the URL, relevant device, observed problem and proposed rerun.
A lab test and a real visitor tell you different things
Use a sample page without enough visitor data to show what you can still investigate.

Questions a buyer might ask

Will every page show real-visitor data?
Google supplies it when there is enough eligible data. The lab result can still give you a starting point.

PageSpeed timings vary and estimated savings concern page performance. Measure conversion impact separately.
