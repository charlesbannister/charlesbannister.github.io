Settings audit

November 20, 2026

An account lands on your desk on Monday. Before you touch a bid or a budget, you need to know which conversions campaigns use, where ads are eligible to show, and which choices need a conversation.

Who needs it, and when?

Start with the PPC lead taking over an account. Their alternative is a checklist and a tour through Google Ads. Your strongest case is showing the setting and the expectation together, with an explanation of why that expectation matters.

A discrepancy is the start of a useful conversation. One campaign may use a different conversion goal because it has a different job. Make room for that answer in the demo. The buyer wants to know which differences are deliberate and which need fixing.

What the Shot does for them

Actual account and enabled campaign settings, grouped by setting
Compare how campaigns are set up across conversion tracking, targeting, bidding and schedules without opening each campaign in turn.
Your standards and severity
Put the expected setting beside the actual one and spend the handover on discrepancies that could affect the account.
CSV, JSON and self-contained HTML export
Give a colleague the findings and suggested fixes to discuss before anyone makes a change.

Draft page copy

Taking over Google Ads? Find out what the last team set up.

Taking over a Google Ads account? See how its campaigns are set up before you change anything. Compare tracking, bidding, targeting and more with your standards, then take the findings into your handover.

What to show in a demo

  1. Use a sample account where two campaigns with similar jobs use different conversion goals.
  2. Show the actual settings, the expected goal, and the discrepancy with its suggested fix.
  3. Explain the business reason that would make the difference intentional, then open the exported report a colleague would receive.

Content angles

The first ten minutes of a Google Ads handover
Lead with the two campaign settings side by side. Demonstrate one discrepancy and turn it into a question for the previous account owner.
When two campaigns should have different settings
Use an FAQ or short post about an intentional exception, then show how standards help put the decision on record.
