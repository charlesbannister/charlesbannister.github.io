Search term trends

Labs · November 20, 2026

A home services marketer notices more enquiries about heat-pump servicing. In an illustrative account, several related queries start appearing regularly before the busy season. Seeing when those searches appeared and which campaigns picked them up helps the marketer choose whether to brief the site team or adjust the next campaign test.

Who needs it, and when?

Write for the person whose search-term review already produces lists but loses the history. Their alternative is reconciling exports or relying on memory of last month’s report. They need to tell whether a particular customer query keeps returning, and what that should change.

Lead with emerging demand. A recurring customer phrase gives paid media and the website team something to discuss in the customer’s words. N-grams helps discover phrases across many queries. This Shot helps follow a specific reported query through time and see which campaigns picked it up.

What the Shot does for them

Reported search terms by week or month with year-earlier periods
See whether a named query is a brief spike, repeats week after week or followed a comparable pattern last year.
Views by triggering match type or campaign and a separate look at Performance Max terms
Trace where advertising picked up the query. That helps you decide where to investigate or test the message.
An Other terms row for terms Google withholds
See how much activity sits outside the named rows before drawing conclusions about the whole account.

Draft page copy

What have people started searching for this month?

See how the searches people actually made changed from week to week. Follow reported queries across campaigns and compare them with the same time last year. Find the searches that keep returning and decide which are worth a closer look.

What to show in a demo

  1. Use an illustrative account and show a named search for heat-pump servicing that appears across several weeks.
  2. Compare the same weeks a year earlier, then see which campaign picked up the search and check the separate Performance Max view where relevant.
  3. Point out the Other terms row before ending with a proposed page or ad test.

Content angles

What customers started asking before the busy season
Walk through one named search and use the weekly view to show when it started returning. Lead with this because it puts a recognisable customer phrase on screen straight away.
Where did last year’s searches go?
Follow a previously steady query through the same weeks this year and use campaign context to plan a delivery check.

Questions a buyer might ask

Does this tell me overall market demand?
It shows queries reported for your own ads. Reach, campaign settings and Google’s reporting limits affect what you see.
Can I see the individual queries in Other terms?
Google withholds those queries. The row gives you their combined activity.

A new named query in the report is a signal worth exploring. Use delivery and market context to test whether interest actually changed.
